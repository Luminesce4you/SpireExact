## Full-suite errors were not hidden

1. `test_dashboard_controls...test_180_minutes_remains_valid_and_entry_records_latest_options`: old dashboard test expects 180 minutes while public UI/ordinary launcher accepts 1–45. The new **explicit research** argument does not silently change this old public contract.
2. `setUpClass (test_solver_patches.SolverPatchContractTests)`: vendor/CombatSolver source absent in the source-only environment.
3. Two Windows-specific sharing tests skip on Linux.

Both errors were independently reproduced on the unmodified baseline; retained original evidence includes `baseline-validation.json`, `baseline-python313.log`, `baseline-ci-python312.log`. They remain errors, not passing tests. No baseline test was removed or rewritten to manufacture a green run.

Cross-scale allocation helps the intended early-commitment trap but pays a cost on late-local problems. The contextual model can chase the wrong damage surrogate even with real rather than synthetic labels. **Its allocation control was consequently removed from the default**, not justified after the fact by cherry-picking an easier world.

For these 30 experiments, measured Python controller/oracle time sums were about 0.77 s for focus-core, 1.08 s for reroot without the model, and 5.46 s for reroot with the model (unequal trajectories and stopping times, therefore not a controlled speedup ratio). The oracle's separately recorded “seconds” are deliberately simulated. No inference about native wall cost follows from those numbers.

## F2 threshold conclusion

The old real-gate and joint regressions use 24 rows / 16 changed rows; joint rows additionally depend on complete 5/10 synthetic F2 samples and can share evidence. Lowering these values may reduce cold-start delay but cannot fix the additive full-HP F2 surrogate or create independent sample directions.

The new exact-prefix witness archive uses a real continuation immediately. The optional grouped contextual model can form a contrast from two distinct real feature groups, and twenty repeated equal projections remain one effective group in tests. Neither is a claim that two samples are sufficient for reliable generalization. Default old thresholds remain unchanged; `i082-low-threshold` isolates an 8/4 alternative.

User-reported >30-minute F1-to-F2 tail cases were not accompanied by a complete raw native log panel here. They motivate the new first-entry/pass/fit clocks and right-censored conversion reports but are not republished as a measured dataset.
